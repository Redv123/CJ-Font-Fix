## What's changed

- Fixed an Advanced-mode case where a page could inject Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Japanese fonts even with experimental mixed-language detection off. Pages now use one selected regional font unless the mixed-page decision activates; specific trusted language declarations remain handled by the browser.

## Install

1. Download `cj-font-fallback-fix-0.9.1.zip` from **Assets** below.
2. Extract the ZIP.
3. Open `chrome://extensions`, enable **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked**, and select the extracted folder.
